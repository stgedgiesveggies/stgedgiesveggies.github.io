---
title: "Radish Greens Pesto"
veggies:
  - red-round-radishes
  - french-breakfast-radishes
  - purple-daikon-radishes
  - watermelon-radish
time: "15 min"
serves: "Makes about 1 cup"
difficulty: "Easy"
ingredients:
  - "1 bunch radish greens"
  - "3/4–1 cup extra-virgin olive oil"
  - "1 cup shredded Parmesan"
  - "1–2 cloves garlic, roughly chopped"
  - "1/2 cup raw or roasted pine nuts, pistachios, almonds, pumpkin seeds, or sunflower seeds"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
steps:
  - "Rough chop the greens and wash well in a bath of water. Drain and spin or towel-dry."
  - "Add about 2/3 of the garlic, Parmesan, olive oil, greens, nuts, some pepper, and a few good pinches of salt to a food processor. Blitz!"
  - "Taste. From here, nudge things in the direction you want — richer with more oil and cheese, extra garlicky, or more greens. Make sure to add enough salt."
  - "Stays Kermit-green without oxidizing. Freezes well."
