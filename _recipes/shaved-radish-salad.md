---
title: "Shaved Radish Salad with Sesame & Citrus"
veggies:
  - watermelon-radish
time: "10 min"
serves: "Serves 2"
difficulty: "Easy"
ingredients:
  - "3–4 watermelon radishes, very thinly sliced"
  - "2 tbsp toasted sesame oil"
  - "1 tbsp rice vinegar"
  - "1 tsp honey"
  - "Flake salt & black pepper"
  - "Optional: toasted sesame seeds, fresh mint"
steps:
  - "Slice radishes as thin as possible on a mandoline or with a sharp knife."
  - "Whisk sesame oil, rice vinegar, and honey. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Toss radishes in dressing. Let sit 5 minutes — they soften slightly and absorb flavor."
  - "Finish with flake salt, sesame seeds, and torn mint."
---
