---
title: "Radish & Goat Cheese Tartine"
veggies:
  - watermelon-radish
time: "15 min"
serves: "Serves 2"
difficulty: "Easy"
ingredients:
  - "4 thick slices sourdough"
  - "4 oz fresh goat cheese, room temperature"
  - "3 watermelon radishes, thinly sliced"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "Fresh dill, lemon zest, flake salt"
steps:
  - "Toast bread until golden and crisp."
  - "Spread generously with goat cheese."
  - "Layer radish slices overlapping across each piece."
  - "Drizzle with olive oil. Scatter dill, lemon zest, and flake salt. Serve immediately."
---
