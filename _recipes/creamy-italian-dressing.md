---
title: "Creamy Italian Dressing"
veggies:
  - purple-daikon-radishes
  - red-round-radishes
  - french-breakfast-radishes
time: "10 min"
serves: "Makes about 1 cup"
difficulty: "Easy"
ingredients:
  - "1/4 cup white wine vinegar"
  - "2 tablespoons lemon juice"
  - "1/3 cup olive oil"
  - "1/2 cup mayonnaise"
  - "2 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 1/2 teaspoons each: dried basil, oregano, parsley, thyme"
  - "2 teaspoons honey"
  - "1/4 cup Parmesan cheese, grated"
  - "Salt and pepper to taste"
steps:
  - "Whisk together all ingredients until well combined."
  - "Taste and adjust seasoning — more lemon for brightness, more honey for sweetness."
  - "Serve over thinly sliced radishes, or use as a dipping sauce or general salad dressing. Makes anything taste great."
---
