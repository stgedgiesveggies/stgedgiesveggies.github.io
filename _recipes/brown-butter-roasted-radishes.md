---
title: "Brown Butter Roasted Radishes"
veggies:
  - watermelon-radish
time: "30 min"
serves: "Serves 3–4"
difficulty: "Medium"
ingredients:
  - "6 watermelon radishes, quartered"
  - "3 tbsp unsalted butter"
  - "1 tsp fresh thyme leaves"
  - "Salt & black pepper"
  - "Optional: drizzle of honey to finish"
steps:
  - "Preheat oven to 425°F."
  - "Toss radish quarters with 2 tbsp melted butter, salt, and pepper. Roast 20–22 min until edges caramelize."
  - "Meanwhile, cook remaining butter in a small pan until golden and nutty, about 3 min."
  - "Drizzle brown butter over roasted radishes. Top with thyme. Finish with honey if you like."
---
