---
title: "Fennel Salad"
veggies:
  - fennel
time: "15 min + 30 min rest"
serves: "Serves 4"
difficulty: "Easy"
ingredients:
  - "3 tablespoons lemon juice"
  - "2 teaspoons Dijon mustard"
  - "2 teaspoons honey"
  - "Salt and pepper"
  - "1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil"
  - "2 fennel bulbs, stalks discarded, bulbs halved, cored and sliced thin"
  - "1/2 red onion, halved through root end and sliced thin"
  - "1/2 cup raisins, chopped"
  - "3 tablespoons capers or olives, rinsed and minced"
  - "1/2 cup fresh parsley or celery leaves"
  - "1/2 cup sliced almonds, toasted"
steps:
  - "Whisk lemon juice, mustard, honey, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1 teaspoon pepper in a large serving bowl until combined."
  - "While whisking constantly, slowly drizzle in oil until combined."
  - "Add fennel, onion, raisins, and capers/olives and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes."
  - "Stir in parsley and almonds and season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve."
---
