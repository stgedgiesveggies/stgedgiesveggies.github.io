---
title: "BLT Salad with Creamy Basil Dressing"
veggies:
  - salad-mix
  - basil
time: "30 min"
serves: "Serves 4"
difficulty: "Easy"
ingredients:
  - "1–2 lbs bacon, fried crispy (save the fat in the pan)"
  - "Large bag salad mix"
  - "2–3 tomatoes, chopped"
  - "4–6 thick slices bread, cubed and fried golden in the bacon fat"
  - "CREAMY BASIL DRESSING: 3 tablespoons olive oil"
  - "1 tablespoon wine vinegar"
  - "Juice of 1 lemon"
  - "Salt and pepper"
  - "1 avocado"
  - "Large handful fresh basil, chopped"
steps:
  - "Fry the bacon until crispy. Remove and drain. Save the fat in the pan."
  - "Fry the bread cubes in the bacon fat until golden and crispy. Set aside."
  - "Blend all dressing ingredients together in a blender or food processor until creamy. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Toss salad mix with dressing. Top with tomatoes, bacon crumbles, and bread croutons. Serve immediately."
---
