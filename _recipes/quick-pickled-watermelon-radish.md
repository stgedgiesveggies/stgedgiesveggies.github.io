---
title: "Quick-Pickled Watermelon Radish"
veggies:
  - watermelon-radish
time: "10 min + overnight"
serves: "Makes 1 jar"
difficulty: "Easy"
ingredients:
  - "4–5 watermelon radishes, sliced into half-moons"
  - "½ cup rice vinegar"
  - "½ cup water"
  - "1 tbsp sugar"
  - "1 tsp kosher salt"
  - "Optional: 1 tsp whole peppercorns, 1 garlic clove"
steps:
  - "Pack radish slices into a clean jar."
  - "Combine vinegar, water, sugar, and salt in a small pot. Heat until sugar dissolves. Let cool 5 minutes."
  - "Pour brine over radishes. Add peppercorns and garlic if using."
  - "Seal and refrigerate overnight. Keeps for 2 weeks. The brine turns vivid pink."
---
