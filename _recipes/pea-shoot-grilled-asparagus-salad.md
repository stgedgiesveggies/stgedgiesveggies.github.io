---
title: "Pea Shoot and Grilled Asparagus Salad"
veggies:
  - pea-shoots
  - asparagus
time: "20 min"
serves: "Serves 4"
difficulty: "Easy"
ingredients:
  - "1 1/2 cups asparagus, grilled then cut into 2-inch pieces"
  - "1 tablespoon olive oil"
  - "1 cup (packed) pea shoots"
  - "4 oz spinach"
  - "1 teaspoon salt"
  - "1/2 teaspoon pepper"
  - "8 radishes, halved"
  - "1/3 cup walnuts, chopped and toasted"
  - "DRESSING: 2 scallions"
  - "1 small garlic clove"
  - "1/2 cup rice or white wine vinegar"
  - "1/2 cup olive oil"
  - "1 1/2 tablespoons honey"
  - "1 teaspoon salt"
  - "1/4 teaspoon pepper"
steps:
  - "Toss asparagus in olive oil and grill over high heat until charred and tender, about 5–7 minutes. Cut into 2-inch pieces."
  - "Blend all dressing ingredients together in a blender or food processor until smooth."
  - "Combine pea shoots, spinach, grilled asparagus, radishes, and walnuts in a large bowl."
  - "Toss with dressing, season with salt and pepper, and serve."
---
