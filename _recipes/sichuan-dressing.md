---
title: "Sichuan Dressing"
veggies:
  - scallions
time: "15 min"
serves: "Makes about 1 1/2 cups"
difficulty: "Medium"
ingredients:
  - "1 teaspoon Sichuan peppercorns"
  - "4 scallions, thinly sliced"
  - "4 garlic cloves, finely chopped"
  - "2 tablespoons white or black sesame seeds"
  - "1 1/2 teaspoons crushed red pepper flakes"
  - "1 cup vegetable oil (grapeseed recommended)"
  - "1/2 cup tamari soy sauce"
  - "3 tablespoons balsamic vinegar"
steps:
  - "Grind the peppercorns and add to a heatproof serving bowl along with the scallions, garlic, sesame seeds, and red pepper flakes."
  - "Heat oil in a small heavy saucepan until you see the first sign of smoke. Carefully pour the hot oil into the bowl with the peppercorn mixture (caution: oil will bubble and rise for a second). This releases the flavors without burning them."
  - "Stir in tamari and balsamic vinegar. Let cool."
  - "Serve over cold noodles, steamed or stir-fried vegetables, or just about anything crunchy and hearty."
---
