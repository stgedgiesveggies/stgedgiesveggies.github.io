---
title: "Beet Greens and Eggs"
veggies:
  - beets
time: "15 min"
serves: "Serves 2"
difficulty: "Easy"
ingredients:
  - "1 bunch beet greens, stems trimmed, roughly chopped"
  - "2 tablespoons butter"
  - "2–3 garlic cloves, chopped"
  - "1/2 teaspoon chili powder"
  - "1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika"
  - "Pinch of ground fennel seed (optional)"
  - "Salt to taste"
  - "2–4 eggs, fried or poached to serve"
steps:
  - "Chop the garlic and roughly chop the greens."
  - "Melt butter in a pan over medium heat. Add the greens along with chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, and ground fennel if using. Stir to combine."
  - "Let the greens wilt down, then add the garlic. Cook, stirring occasionally, until most of the greens' liquid evaporates."
  - "Serve alongside fried eggs. Goes great with a sesame bagel."
---
