---
title: "Spring Roll Salad with Sesame Miso Dressing"
veggies:
  - salad-turnips
  - watermelon-radish
  - beets
  - salad-mix
time: "30 min"
serves: "Serves 4"
difficulty: "Medium"
ingredients:
  - "A mix of shredded vegetables: cabbage, red pepper, cucumber, beets, salad turnips, watermelon radishes"
  - "8 oz wheat spaghetti, broken in half and cooked"
  - "Grilled chicken, thinly sliced (optional)"
  - "Thinly sliced fresh chiles"
  - "Fresh cilantro"
  - "Sunflower shoots"
  - "Sesame seeds"
  - "DRESSING: 1 tablespoon finely diced onion"
  - "1 minced garlic clove"
  - "2 teaspoons rice vinegar"
  - "1 teaspoon honey"
  - "1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil"
  - "3 teaspoons canola oil or other neutral oil"
  - "2 teaspoons soy sauce"
  - "1 tablespoon white miso paste"
  - "1 tablespoon water"
  - "1 tablespoon sesame seeds"
  - "1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro"
steps:
  - "Shred the vegetables using a mandolin or sharp knife."
  - "Cook the broken spaghetti noodles according to package directions. Drain and cool."
  - "Blend all dressing ingredients together until smooth."
  - "Toss the shredded vegetables and noodles with the dressing."
  - "Top with grilled chicken, thinly sliced chiles, fresh cilantro, sunflower shoots, and sesame seeds."
---
