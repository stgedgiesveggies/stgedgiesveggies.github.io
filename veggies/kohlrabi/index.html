Kohlrabi

Kohlrabi is a funny looking vegetable — the main attraction is the ballooning stem. Tear off the leaves, peel the skin, and you have a sweet, crunchy, white orb to do with as you wish. It might be best to slice and enjoy lightly salted with cheese and crackers, or julienned on a salad. The leaves can be enjoyed like collards or broccoli greens, which take well to a patient simmer. A rare and special offering when we have it.

Flavor Sweet, mild
Texture Crisp, crunchy
Season Summer
Pairs with Salt · Cheese · Vinaigrette · Caraway
