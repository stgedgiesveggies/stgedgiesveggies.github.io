Butterhead Lettuce

Butterhead is the lettuce that tastes like lettuce is supposed to taste. Soft, sweet, a little grassy. The leaves cup naturally — perfect for wraps or torn over anything. Store loosely in the fridge and eat within a few days.

Flavor Sweet, mild
Texture Tender, buttery
Season Spring & Fall
Pairs with Vinaigrette · Herbs · Radishes · Soft cheese
