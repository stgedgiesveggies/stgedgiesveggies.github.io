Fresh Garlic

Fresh green garlic — immature garlic with apple-crisp cloves and a skin that hasn't dried yet. The only difference from cured garlic is the freshness. Extract the cloves per usual; the outer layers work nicely as a stock ingredient. Use it as you would green onions: puree raw or grilled into a creamy dressing, garnish a light soup, or toss into a salad. Leave unpeeled on the counter and it will naturally cure in a few weeks.

Flavor Garlicy, fresh, mild
Texture Firm, crisp
Season Early Summer
Pairs with Olive oil · Lemon · Herbs · Butter
