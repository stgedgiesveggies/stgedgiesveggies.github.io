Fennel

Fennel is a crisp bulb that grows above the ground with a celery-like texture and a licorice flavor — used heavily in Italian cooking from soups and sauces to salads and sautés. It's useful from base to fronds: slice the bulb thin for salads, roast it with other vegetables, or chop the feathery fronds for garnish and sauces. A fall crop that gets better as the season cools.

Flavor Licorice, sweet, aromatic
Texture Crisp, juicy
Season Fall
Pairs with Lemon · Olive oil · Dill · Almonds · Feta

Fennel Salad

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —3 tablespoons lemon juice
  • —2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
  • —2 teaspoons honey
  • —Salt and pepper
  • —1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • —2 fennel bulbs, stalks discarded, bulbs halved, cored and sliced thin
  • —1/2 red onion, halved through root end and sliced thin
  • —1/2 cup raisins, chopped
  • —3 tablespoons capers or olives, rinsed and minced
  • —1/2 cup fresh parsley or celery leaves
  • —1/2 cup sliced almonds, toasted
Method
  1. 1 Whisk lemon juice, mustard, honey, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1 teaspoon pepper in a large serving bowl until combined.
  2. 2 While whisking constantly, slowly drizzle in oil until combined.
  3. 3 Add fennel, onion, raisins, and capers/olives and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  4. 4 Stir in parsley and almonds and season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve.

Shaved Fennel & Beet Greens Salad

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —1 medium bulb of fennel, cored and shaved or sliced very thin
  • —10–12 stems of beet greens, stems trimmed, leaves torn
  • —3 tablespoons fennel fronds, finely chopped
  • —3 tablespoons fresh dill, finely chopped
  • —1 1/2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • —1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil or a nut oil (walnut or hazelnut)
  • —Drizzle of honey
  • —1–2 oz feta cheese, crumbled
  • —1/3 cup toasted nuts (hazelnuts, walnuts, almonds, or pine nuts), chopped
  • —Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
Method
  1. 1 Shave the fennel bulb very thin using a mandolin or sharp knife.
  2. 2 Tear the beet green leaves into bite-sized pieces.
  3. 3 Whisk together lemon juice, olive oil, and honey. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. 4 Toss fennel, beet greens, fennel fronds, and dill with the dressing.
  5. 5 Top with feta and toasted nuts. Serve immediately.
