Salad Mix

Salad mix is what we built the farm around. A seasonal blend of loose-leaf lettuces, baby arugula, pea shoots, and sunflower shoots — harvested in the morning and washed twice before it reaches you. It's fresher than anything you'll find at a grocery store, and it shows. People who tell us they don't like salad change their minds after trying this. Store with air in the bag, in the fridge, and shake the leaves gently before each use.

Flavor Fresh, mild with a savory note
Texture Tender, varied
Season Spring through Fall
Pairs with Olive oil · Vinegar · Lemon · Radishes · Herbs

BLT Salad with Creamy Basil Dressing

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —1–2 lbs bacon, fried crispy (save the fat in the pan)
  • —Large bag salad mix
  • —2–3 tomatoes, chopped
  • —4–6 thick slices bread, cubed and fried golden in the bacon fat
  • —CREAMY BASIL DRESSING: 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • —1 tablespoon wine vinegar
  • —Juice of 1 lemon
  • —Salt and pepper
  • —1 avocado
  • —Large handful fresh basil, chopped
Method
  1. 1 Fry the bacon until crispy. Remove and drain. Save the fat in the pan.
  2. 2 Fry the bread cubes in the bacon fat until golden and crispy. Set aside.
  3. 3 Blend all dressing ingredients together in a blender or food processor until creamy. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. 4 Toss salad mix with dressing. Top with tomatoes, bacon crumbles, and bread croutons. Serve immediately.

Spring Roll Salad with Sesame Miso Dressing

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —A mix of shredded vegetables: cabbage, red pepper, cucumber, beets, salad turnips, watermelon radishes
  • —8 oz wheat spaghetti, broken in half and cooked
  • —Grilled chicken, thinly sliced (optional)
  • —Thinly sliced fresh chiles
  • —Fresh cilantro
  • —Sunflower shoots
  • —Sesame seeds
  • —DRESSING: 1 tablespoon finely diced onion
  • —1 minced garlic clove
  • —2 teaspoons rice vinegar
  • —1 teaspoon honey
  • —1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • —3 teaspoons canola oil or other neutral oil
  • —2 teaspoons soy sauce
  • —1 tablespoon white miso paste
  • —1 tablespoon water
  • —1 tablespoon sesame seeds
  • —1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro
Method
  1. 1 Shred the vegetables using a mandolin or sharp knife.
  2. 2 Cook the broken spaghetti noodles according to package directions. Drain and cool.
  3. 3 Blend all dressing ingredients together until smooth.
  4. 4 Toss the shredded vegetables and noodles with the dressing.
  5. 5 Top with grilled chicken, thinly sliced chiles, fresh cilantro, sunflower shoots, and sesame seeds.
