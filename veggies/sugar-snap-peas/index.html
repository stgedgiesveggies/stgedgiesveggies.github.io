Sugar Snap Peas

Sugar snaps are the best argument for eating vegetables raw. Sweet, crisp, and juicy straight from the vine. String them if the pods are mature, or just snap and eat. They also stir-fry beautifully in under two minutes.

Flavor Sweet, fresh
Texture Crisp, juicy
Season Spring
Pairs with Mint · Sesame · Ricotta · Lemon
