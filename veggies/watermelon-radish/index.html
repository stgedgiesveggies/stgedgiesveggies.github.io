Watermelon Radish

Don't let the plain exterior fool you — inside, rings of hot pink and white. Mild, crisp, a little peppery. Holds crunch raw, softens beautifully roasted, turns glossy when pickled.

Flavor Mild, peppery
Texture Crisp, juicy
Season Spring & Fall
Pairs with Sesame · Citrus · Miso · Goat cheese

Recipes

30 min Serves 3–4 Medium

Brown Butter Roasted Radishes

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —6 watermelon radishes, quartered
  • —3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • —1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • —Salt & black pepper
  • —Optional: drizzle of honey to finish
Method
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2 Toss radish quarters with 2 tbsp melted butter, salt, and pepper. Roast 20–22 min until edges caramelize.
  3. 3 Meanwhile, cook remaining butter in a small pan until golden and nutty, about 3 min.
  4. 4 Drizzle brown butter over roasted radishes. Top with thyme. Finish with honey if you like.
10 min + overnight Makes 1 jar Easy

Quick-Pickled Watermelon Radish

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —4–5 watermelon radishes, sliced into half-moons
  • —½ cup rice vinegar
  • —½ cup water
  • —1 tbsp sugar
  • —1 tsp kosher salt
  • —Optional: 1 tsp whole peppercorns, 1 garlic clove
Method
  1. 1 Pack radish slices into a clean jar.
  2. 2 Combine vinegar, water, sugar, and salt in a small pot. Heat until sugar dissolves. Let cool 5 minutes.
  3. 3 Pour brine over radishes. Add peppercorns and garlic if using.
  4. 4 Seal and refrigerate overnight. Keeps for 2 weeks. The brine turns vivid pink.
15 min Serves 2 Easy

Radish & Goat Cheese Tartine

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —4 thick slices sourdough
  • —4 oz fresh goat cheese, room temperature
  • —3 watermelon radishes, thinly sliced
  • —2 tbsp olive oil
  • —Fresh dill, lemon zest, flake salt
Method
  1. 1 Toast bread until golden and crisp.
  2. 2 Spread generously with goat cheese.
  3. 3 Layer radish slices overlapping across each piece.
  4. 4 Drizzle with olive oil. Scatter dill, lemon zest, and flake salt. Serve immediately.

Radish Greens Pesto

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —1 bunch radish greens
  • —3/4–1 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • —1 cup shredded Parmesan
  • —1–2 cloves garlic, roughly chopped
  • —1/2 cup raw or roasted pine nuts, pistachios, almonds, pumpkin seeds, or sunflower seeds
  • —Salt and pepper to taste
Method
  1. 1 Rough chop the greens and wash well in a bath of water. Drain and spin or towel-dry.
  2. 2 Add about 2/3 of the garlic, Parmesan, olive oil, greens, nuts, some pepper, and a few good pinches of salt to a food processor. Blitz!
  3. 3 Taste. From here, nudge things in the direction you want — richer with more oil and cheese, extra garlicky, or more greens. Make sure to add enough salt.
  4. 4 Stays Kermit-green without oxidizing. Freezes well.
10 min Serves 2 Easy

Shaved Radish Salad with Sesame & Citrus

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —3–4 watermelon radishes, very thinly sliced
  • —2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • —1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • —1 tsp honey
  • —Flake salt & black pepper
  • —Optional: toasted sesame seeds, fresh mint
Method
  1. 1 Slice radishes as thin as possible on a mandoline or with a sharp knife.
  2. 2 Whisk sesame oil, rice vinegar, and honey. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. 3 Toss radishes in dressing. Let sit 5 minutes — they soften slightly and absorb flavor.
  4. 4 Finish with flake salt, sesame seeds, and torn mint.
30 min Serves 4 Medium

Spring Roll Salad with Sesame Miso Dressing

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —A mix of shredded vegetables: cabbage, red pepper, cucumber, beets, salad turnips, watermelon radishes
  • —8 oz wheat spaghetti, broken in half and cooked
  • —Grilled chicken, thinly sliced (optional)
  • —Thinly sliced fresh chiles
  • —Fresh cilantro
  • —Sunflower shoots
  • —Sesame seeds
  • —DRESSING: 1 tablespoon finely diced onion
  • —1 minced garlic clove
  • —2 teaspoons rice vinegar
  • —1 teaspoon honey
  • —1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • —3 teaspoons canola oil or other neutral oil
  • —2 teaspoons soy sauce
  • —1 tablespoon white miso paste
  • —1 tablespoon water
  • —1 tablespoon sesame seeds
  • —1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro
Method
  1. 1 Shred the vegetables using a mandolin or sharp knife.
  2. 2 Cook the broken spaghetti noodles according to package directions. Drain and cool.
  3. 3 Blend all dressing ingredients together until smooth.
  4. 4 Toss the shredded vegetables and noodles with the dressing.
  5. 5 Top with grilled chicken, thinly sliced chiles, fresh cilantro, sunflower shoots, and sesame seeds.
