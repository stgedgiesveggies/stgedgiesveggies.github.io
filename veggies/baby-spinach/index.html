Baby Spinach

Fresh-from-the-garden spinach is sweet and delicious unlike any other time of year — especially when it's overwintered and kissed by cold nights, which push the sugars higher and higher. Beautiful and full of flavor in a way store-bought spinach simply isn't. Eat it raw in salads, wilt it into eggs, or blend it into a smoothie. It won't last long.

Flavor Sweet, mild
Texture Tender, silky
Season Spring & Fall
Pairs with Lemon · Garlic · Feta · Strawberries
