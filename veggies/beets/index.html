Beets

We grow the Badger Flame variety — regal and seriously delicious raw. All the classic beet flavor without the earthiness that puts some folks off beets. They're a remarkable variety bred at the University of Wisconsin, vivid orange-yellow within, and especially lovely eaten raw, sliced thin or grated over a salad. The greens are fantastic too — perfect for any recipe that calls for hearty greens. Don't throw them away.

Flavor Sweet, earthy, bold
Texture Firm, dense
Season Summer & Fall
Pairs with Goat cheese · Walnuts · Orange · Balsamic

Beet Greens and Eggs

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —1 bunch beet greens, stems trimmed, roughly chopped
  • —2 tablespoons butter
  • —2–3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • —1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • —1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • —Pinch of ground fennel seed (optional)
  • —Salt to taste
  • —2–4 eggs, fried or poached to serve
Method
  1. 1 Chop the garlic and roughly chop the greens.
  2. 2 Melt butter in a pan over medium heat. Add the greens along with chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, and ground fennel if using. Stir to combine.
  3. 3 Let the greens wilt down, then add the garlic. Cook, stirring occasionally, until most of the greens' liquid evaporates.
  4. 4 Serve alongside fried eggs. Goes great with a sesame bagel.

Shaved Fennel & Beet Greens Salad

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —1 medium bulb of fennel, cored and shaved or sliced very thin
  • —10–12 stems of beet greens, stems trimmed, leaves torn
  • —3 tablespoons fennel fronds, finely chopped
  • —3 tablespoons fresh dill, finely chopped
  • —1 1/2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • —1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil or a nut oil (walnut or hazelnut)
  • —Drizzle of honey
  • —1–2 oz feta cheese, crumbled
  • —1/3 cup toasted nuts (hazelnuts, walnuts, almonds, or pine nuts), chopped
  • —Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
Method
  1. 1 Shave the fennel bulb very thin using a mandolin or sharp knife.
  2. 2 Tear the beet green leaves into bite-sized pieces.
  3. 3 Whisk together lemon juice, olive oil, and honey. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. 4 Toss fennel, beet greens, fennel fronds, and dill with the dressing.
  5. 5 Top with feta and toasted nuts. Serve immediately.

Spring Roll Salad with Sesame Miso Dressing

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —A mix of shredded vegetables: cabbage, red pepper, cucumber, beets, salad turnips, watermelon radishes
  • —8 oz wheat spaghetti, broken in half and cooked
  • —Grilled chicken, thinly sliced (optional)
  • —Thinly sliced fresh chiles
  • —Fresh cilantro
  • —Sunflower shoots
  • —Sesame seeds
  • —DRESSING: 1 tablespoon finely diced onion
  • —1 minced garlic clove
  • —2 teaspoons rice vinegar
  • —1 teaspoon honey
  • —1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • —3 teaspoons canola oil or other neutral oil
  • —2 teaspoons soy sauce
  • —1 tablespoon white miso paste
  • —1 tablespoon water
  • —1 tablespoon sesame seeds
  • —1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro
Method
  1. 1 Shred the vegetables using a mandolin or sharp knife.
  2. 2 Cook the broken spaghetti noodles according to package directions. Drain and cool.
  3. 3 Blend all dressing ingredients together until smooth.
  4. 4 Toss the shredded vegetables and noodles with the dressing.
  5. 5 Top with grilled chicken, thinly sliced chiles, fresh cilantro, sunflower shoots, and sesame seeds.
