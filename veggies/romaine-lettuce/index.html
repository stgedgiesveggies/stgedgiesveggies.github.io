Romaine Lettuce

Romaine is a newer crop for us, and we are happy it's here. These heads are sturdy, deeply green, and crisp in a way that holds up to heavier dressings and heartier toppings. Great for a classic Caesar, piled onto a sandwich, or torn up for a salad that won't wilt before you finish it.

Flavor Mild, slightly sweet
Texture Crisp, sturdy
Season Spring, Summer & Fall
Pairs with Caesar dressing · Parmesan · Lemon · Croutons
