Pea Shoots

Pea shoots are the tender taste of spring — bursting with Springy goodness, antioxidant-packed, and very tender. On their own they are a versatile kitchen ingredient: enjoy them raw in a spring roll, wilted into a creamy pasta dish, or blended into a simple potato onion soup. We grow ours directly in the ground under the sun, which makes them far more nutritious than anything grown under lights indoors. Snack on them standing at the stall.

Flavor Sweet, fresh, grassy
Texture Tender, delicate
Season Spring & early Summer
Pairs with Sesame · Lemon · Avocado · Mint

Pea Shoot and Grilled Asparagus Salad

↓ See recipe ↑ Hide recipe
Ingredients
  • —1 1/2 cups asparagus, grilled then cut into 2-inch pieces
  • —1 tablespoon olive oil
  • —1 cup (packed) pea shoots
  • —4 oz spinach
  • —1 teaspoon salt
  • —1/2 teaspoon pepper
  • —8 radishes, halved
  • —1/3 cup walnuts, chopped and toasted
  • —DRESSING: 2 scallions
  • —1 small garlic clove
  • —1/2 cup rice or white wine vinegar
  • —1/2 cup olive oil
  • —1 1/2 tablespoons honey
  • —1 teaspoon salt
  • —1/4 teaspoon pepper
Method
  1. 1 Toss asparagus in olive oil and grill over high heat until charred and tender, about 5–7 minutes. Cut into 2-inch pieces.
  2. 2 Blend all dressing ingredients together in a blender or food processor until smooth.
  3. 3 Combine pea shoots, spinach, grilled asparagus, radishes, and walnuts in a large bowl.
  4. 4 Toss with dressing, season with salt and pepper, and serve.
