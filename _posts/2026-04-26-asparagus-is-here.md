The asparagus bed is up. Three years of waiting — I planted crowns in spring of 2023, left them alone in 2024, left them mostly alone in 2025, and now here we are. Worth every year of it.

Asparagus has the shortest window of anything I grow. Once the soil warms past 50°, the spears come up faster than you can harvest them, and then the plant wants to fern out and put its energy into next year. You get maybe two good weeks of picking before you have to let it go. We're in week one right now.

This Saturday I'll have pencil-thin spears — the kind that don't need peeling, just snap off the woody end and you're done. Roast them at high heat with olive oil and salt for ten minutes. That's all they need.

First-come. I won't have more than a few bundles.
