What a week at the garden. After two cold, soggy weeks that had us worried about the Salanova heads stalling out, warmer nights finally arrived and the beds *took off*. We’re back to cutting gorgeous full heads — the Muir and Flashy Trout’s Back varieties in particular are looking exceptional right now.

## What We’re Bringing Saturday

* **Salanova lettuce heads**

   — mixed varieties, very limited quantity so arrive early
* **Easter Egg radishes**

   — sweet, crisp, zero bite; these have been a surprise hit
* **Baby arugula**

   — slightly spicy with that classic peppery finish
* **Braising mix**

   — kale, chard, and mustard greens, great for a quick weeknight sauté
* **Pea shoots**

   — tender and sweet, back in full force after the weather cleared

We’ll have roughly 40 heads of Salanova this week. If you want one set aside, reply to this email and we’ll hold it under the table for you until 10am.

## From the Farm

The radishes took us a little by surprise — we seeded them as a cover crop filler and they’ve done *so well* we ended up harvesting a full flat. Sometimes the garden has better ideas than we do.

We’ve also started hardening off the first round of tomato starts (Sungold and Brandywine) in preparation for a late-May transplant. No tomatoes at market yet, but it’s nice to see them out in the light.

See you Saturday at the St. Paul Farmers’ Market, 290 5th St. East. We’re usually set up by 7:30am.

— Edgie
