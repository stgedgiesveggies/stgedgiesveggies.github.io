---

Pulled the last of the watermelon radishes this week and they are exactly as dramatic as I hoped. Plain tan on the outside, then you slice one open and there's that hot-pink ring staring back at you. I've been eating them with salt and butter and nothing else.

They're mild this time of year — the cold nights kept them from getting peppery. Slice them thin and they're almost sweet. Slice them thick and there's just a little bite at the end. Either way, the color is the whole point. Put them on a plate and people ask questions.

I've got about four flats left, so this Saturday will likely be the last of them until fall. Sugar snap peas are right behind them — the vines hit the top wire two days ago and the first pods are just starting to swell. Those should be at the stall by late May.

See you Saturday at stall #14.
