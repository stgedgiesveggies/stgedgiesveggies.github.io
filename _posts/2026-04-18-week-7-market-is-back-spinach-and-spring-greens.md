We made it. After a longer-than-usual winter hiatus, we're back at the St. Paul Farmers' Market this Saturday and the cold frames have been quietly doing their thing for the past month. It's not a full table yet, but what we have is *really* good.

## What We're Bringing Saturday

* **Baby spinach**

   — tender, sweet leaves, harvested Thursday morning; this is the first real outdoor cut of the season

* **Spring mix**

   — a blend we seed tightly and cut young; mild and slightly sweet with good texture

* **Tatsoi**

   — dark, spoon-shaped leaves with a mild mustard flavor; great raw or wilted in a pan

* **Chives**

   — bunched, just waking up; excellent on eggs, in potato dishes, or anywhere you'd use a mild onion flavor

This is a lighter table than we'll have in a few weeks — we're being honest about that. But everything is at peak freshness and cut-to-order fresh.

## From the Farm

The cold frames have been earning their keep. We put them down in mid-February over a bed of spinach and spring mix, and by early April they were ready to cut. This week's harvest is the second cut on that same planting, which is always the best — the regrowth after a first cut tends to come in sweeter and more uniform.

Indoors, the greenhouse is getting crowded in the best way. Tomatoes (Sungold, Brandywine, and a new-to-us paste variety called Speckled Roman) are under lights alongside a flat of basil and the first pepper starts. We're still about six weeks out from transplanting anything outside, but it's good to see the seedlings moving.

We've also seeded our first outdoor radish bed of the year. Fingers crossed for two weeks of mild weather.

See you Saturday at the St. Paul Farmers' Market, 290 5th St. East — we'll be set up by 7:30am.
